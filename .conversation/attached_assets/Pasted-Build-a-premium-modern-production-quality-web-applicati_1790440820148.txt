==================================================
1. PROJECT PURPOSE
==================================================

The problem:

Students often waste time checking timetables, asking classmates, searching for classrooms, and figuring out which lecture is currently happening.

The solution:

A centralized student class-finder platform where a student enters their enrollment number and instantly sees:

• Current lecture
• Subject
• Professor
• Classroom / room number
• Lecture time
• Lecture/practical status
• Next lecture
• Weekly timetable
• Their automatically detected branch
• Their semester
• Their batch/division/group

The primary goal is:

“Tell me where my class is right now.”

The entire UX should be optimized around this.
